The identity is drawn on a square pixel grid rather than as freeform curves. Both
the mark and the logotype are generated from that grid at build time, so they can
never drift apart.

## Mark

The monogram sets a **Y** and a **K** on an 8 x 5 grid. Diagonal strokes step one
cell at a time, which is what gives the letterforms their chamfered corners.

## Logotype

The logotype pairs the mark with the wordmark set in the same pixel family. Letters
are drawn on a finer grid than the mark so both share one cap height.

## Clear space

Maintain a clear space of one half the mark's height on all sides, except where the
logo stays clearly recognizable and legible without it.

## Colour

The mark is monochrome and inherits `currentColor`. Use the foreground colour of the
surface it sits on; do not recolour, rotate, outline, or add effects to it.
